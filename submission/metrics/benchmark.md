# Benchmark Evidence

AURALIS was evaluated on 90 controlled speech-noise mixtures:
- 10 speech files
- 3 noise classes: stationary, non-stationary, impulsive
- 3 target SNR conditions: +5, 0, -5 dB

Aggregate validated results:
- 90/90 successful inference cases
- Î”SNR: +12.4529 dB
- Î”STOI: +0.0679
- Î”PESQ: +0.9238
- Î”SI-SDR: +13.7386 dB
- Offline aggregate RTF: approximately 0.0609

These are controlled offline benchmark results. They are not a claim of guaranteed end-to-end 10 ms embedded real-time performance.
